    What's real on this page

    Live answers. The chat sends your conversation to this site's own server, which asks Claude to answer using only A Lovely Touch of Clean's guide: its services, its rates and how to book. Every reply is written fresh, and the cards on the right fill from that same guide.

    Real prices, honest totals. $80 covers the first hour and $25 covers each hour after. The agent can do that math for you, but how long a job takes is confirmed by the team at your free estimate, before any work starts.

    What it can't do. It can't book a date, check the calendar, send a message or take payment. To book, call or text 480-246-7507 or email service@lovelytouchcleaning.com.

    Talk instead of typing. Tap the microphone, speak, and your words appear in the box so you can check them before you send. Your browser turns speech into text, and depending on the browser that can happen on its maker's servers (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari). Where a browser doesn't support it, the microphone hides itself.

    Your conversation stays in this tab. It's kept in your browser only until you close the tab, and nothing is saved to an account. Please don't share payment details or door codes in the chat.
